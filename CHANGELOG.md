# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-07

Initial public-design package:

- complete positioned two-level FreeCAD assembly
- 23 unique print-ready STEP/STL part pairs with authoritative quantities
- table anchor, lower/upper platforms and laminated structural trusses
- serviceable 15-step plus 5-step NeoPixel staircase system
- integrated controller and five-output fixed-5-V building distributor
- reproducible STM32F401 firmware build and prebuilt TinyUF2 image
- assembly, printing, electronics, BOM and licensing documentation
- final day/night gallery, construction sequence and staircase animation
- measured five-building electrical reference of approximately 1.75 A at 5 V

Publication media does not change the mechanical design version.
